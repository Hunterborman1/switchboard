Semi-annual exec sponsor meeting covered 2027 expansion plans, AI Voice Agent pilot results, and two operational issues Nate raised. - Patricia said 2027 planning is centered on expanding AI Voice Agent beyond after-hours claims into FNOL and policy servicing. She wants a phased proposal that shows seat impact and expected containment by queue.

- 2027 budget cycle closes in February; Patricia wants the expansion proposal by mid-January so it can go through their internal review. Nate will be the operational point of contact for scoping.
- Google Ads and Adobe Analytics integrations are fine; no changes requested. Salesforce and Genesys connections are also stable.
- Patricia noted the exec sponsor cadence is working well and asked to keep the semi-annual rhythm through 2027.
